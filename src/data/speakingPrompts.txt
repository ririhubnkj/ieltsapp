**1**
Title: Hometown
Type: Speaking Part 1
- Where is your hometown?
- What do you like most about your hometown?
- Has your hometown changed much since you were a child?
- Would you like to live there in the future?

**2**
Title: Work or Studies
Type: Speaking Part 1
- Do you work or are you a student?
- Why did you choose that subject?
- What do you find most difficult about your studies?
- What would you like to do after you graduate?

**3**
Title: Free Time

- What do you like to do in your free time?
- Do you prefer to spend your free time alone or with other people?
- Have your hobbies changed since you were younger?
- Is there a new hobby you would like to try?

**5**
Title: A person who has influenced you
Type: Speaking Part 2 (1 minute to prepare, 1–2 minutes to speak)
Describe a person who has influenced you.
You should say:
- who this person is
- how you know this person
- what this person has done
- and explain how this person has influenced you.

- what you did there
- and explain why you would like to go back there.
