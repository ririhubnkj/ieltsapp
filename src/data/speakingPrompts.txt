**1**
Title: Hometown
Type: Speaking Part 1
- Where is your hometown?
- What do you like most about your hometown?
- Has your hometown changed much since you were a child?
- Would you like to live there in the future?

**2**
Title: Work or Studies
Type: Speaking Part 1
- Do you work or are you a student?
- Why did you choose that subject?
- What do you find most difficult about your studies?
- What would you like to do after you graduate?

**3**
Title: Free Time
Type: Speaking Part 1
- What do you like to do in your free time?
- Do you prefer to spend your free time alone or with other people?
- Have your hobbies changed since you were younger?
- Is there a new hobby you would like to try?

**4**
Title: Food
Type: Speaking Part 1
- What kind of food do you like?
- Do you often cook at home?
- Is there any food you didn't like as a child but enjoy now?
- How have people's eating habits changed in your country?

**5**
Title: A person who has influenced you
Type: Speaking Part 2 (1 minute to prepare, 1–2 minutes to speak)
Describe a person who has influenced you.
You should say:
- who this person is
- how you know this person
- what this person has done
- and explain how this person has influenced you.

**6**
Title: A place you would like to go back to
Type: Speaking Part 2 (1 minute to prepare, 1–2 minutes to speak)
Describe a place you visited that you would like to go back to.
You should say:
- where this place is
- when you went there
- what you did there
- and explain why you would like to go back there.

**7**
Title: A skill that took a long time to learn
Type: Speaking Part 2 (1 minute to prepare, 1–2 minutes to speak)
Describe a skill you learned that took a long time.
You should say:
- what the skill is
- when you started learning it
- how you learned it
- and explain why it took a long time to learn.

**8**
Title: A time you helped someone
Type: Speaking Part 2 (1 minute to prepare, 1–2 minutes to speak)
Describe a time you helped someone.
You should say:
- when this happened
- who you helped
- how you helped this person
- and explain how you felt about helping them.

**9**
Title: Role models
Type: Speaking Part 3
- What kind of people do young people look up to these days?
- Do you think famous people have a responsibility to be good role models?
- Are parents or teachers more important as role models for children?

**10**
Title: Tourism and travel
Type: Speaking Part 3
- Why do you think people like to travel to other countries?
- What are the advantages and disadvantages of tourism for local communities?
- How do you think travel will change in the future?

**11**
Title: Learning and education
Type: Speaking Part 3
- Is it better to learn a skill from a teacher or by yourself?
- Why do some people give up when they are learning something new?
- Should schools focus more on practical skills than on academic subjects?

**12**
Title: Helping others
Type: Speaking Part 3
- Why do some people do volunteer work?
- Should students be required to do volunteer work at school?
- Do you think people today are less willing to help strangers than in the past?
